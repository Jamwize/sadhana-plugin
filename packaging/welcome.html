Sadhana

A clarinet voice and living drone for Ableton Live, made for you by Sam.

This installs the plug-in for every user on this Mac. When it finishes:

  1. Open Ableton Live.
  2. Go to Settings > Plug-Ins and click Rescan.
  3. Find Sadhana under Plug-Ins > Audio Units > Sam Green.

Om shanti.
