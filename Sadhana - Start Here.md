# Sadhana — Start Here

A clarinet voice and living drone for Ableton Live 12. Made for Dad by Sam.

## 1. Install (once)

1. Double-click **Sadhana Installer.pkg**.
2. If your Mac says it can't check the file:
   - Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
   - Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to "Sadhana Installer".
   - Enter your Mac password.
3. Click through the installer.
4. Open Ableton. Go to **Settings → Plug-Ins** and click **Rescan**.

## 2. Set up your clarinet track (once)

1. Plug in your mic. Make a new **Audio Track** (Cmd+T).
2. Set the track's input to your mic, and set **Monitor** to **In**.
3. In the browser on the left, open **Plug-Ins → Audio Units → Sam Green → Sadhana**. Drag it onto the clarinet track.
4. Play. You'll hear your clarinet and the drone together.

Tip: save this as your default set (**Settings → File & Folder → Save Current Set as Default**). Then Sadhana is ready every time you open Ableton.

## 3. Play

- **Pick an offering** along the top. Each one is a complete sound:
  - **Morning Sadhana:** bright and open
  - **Temple Hall:** long stone reverb
  - **Desert Hijaz:** Arabic colour
  - **Warm Room:** close and intimate
  - **Earth Offering:** for bass clarinet
  - **Sound Bath:** pure overtones at A = 432
- **Choose a key and scale** under the wheel. The drone and the wheel follow them.
- **Watch the wheel while you play.** A bead shows the note you're playing. It turns **green** when you're in tune and in the scale.
- **Hover over any knob** and the bottom line explains what it does.
- **Double-click any knob** to reset it.

## 4. The controls

**Your Voice**
- **Clarinet / Bass Clarinet:** choose which instrument you're playing.
- **Warmth:** analogue glow.
- **Body:** fullness.
- **Air:** breath and sparkle.
- **Smooth:** evens out loud and soft notes.

**The Drone**
- **Drone On / Off.**
- **Low / Mid:** where the drone sits.
- **Drone Level:** how loud the drone is.
- **Overtones:** the scale's natural harmonics, shimmering above the drone note.
- **Texture:** from pure singing-bowl tones to a reedy shruti box.

**The Space**
- **Space:** temple reverb.
- **Echo:** soft repeats in time with Ableton's tempo.
- **Output:** overall volume.

**Note Names:** set to **Bb Clarinet** and every note name on the wheel, and the tuner, is shown as written for your clarinet. The drone key menu is always in concert pitch. The line under the wheel translates it for you, e.g. "Drone in D concert = E on your clarinet".

## Good to know

- When you record, Ableton records your clean clarinet. Sadhana adds the voice and drone as it plays back, so you can change the sound later.
- To make a finished file, use **File → Export Audio/Video**. That captures everything, drone included.
